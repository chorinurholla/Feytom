Choosing lashing

Choose lashing when you need a flexible restraint that will not mark a load, and when you would rather cut to length than carry fixed-length assemblies.

Choosing chains

The grade and diameter determine the application. Grade 70 is a transport chain; Grade 43 is a general-purpose high-test chain. Confirm the correct grade for your use against the manufacturer's documentation.

Choosing rope

Choose rope where you need a light, low-cost line for handling and general securing rather than a load-restraint chain or belt.

Technical and safety documentation

Load limits, working-load limits and safety certifications must be confirmed against the manufacturer's technical documentation before use. A product marking alone may not establish a certified working-load limit.
