Technical and safety documentation

The 4,700 lb working load limit above is the figure printed on this product's packaging. Confirm it against the manufacturer's technical documentation before use, and do not exceed it. This chain is not for overhead lifting. Wear ANSI-approved eye protection during setup and use, inspect the chain before every use, do not use it if damaged, and keep it away from children.
